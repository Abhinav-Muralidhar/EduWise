What is EduWise?

RAG-GROUNDED EDUCATIONAL STUDIO

Most AI study tools suffer from the same fatal flaw: they summarize everything through generic web pretraining, hallucinate made-up facts, and lack the specific context of your actual professor's syllabus, definitions, or exam format.

EduWise flips this paradigm completely. Using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), EduWise indexes your uploaded course textbooks, lecture notes, and study PDFs into semantic 768-dimensional vector spaces. When you request a quiz, presentation, or flashcard deck, EduWise extracts the exact relevant passages and strictly grounds the AI generation on your real course syllabus.

Boundary-Aware Chunking

Documents are parsed sentence-by-sentence and paragraph-by-paragraph with 15% sliding semantic overlap to preserve complete conceptual context.

pgvector Semantic Search

High-performance cosine distance querying retrieves the top-5 most relevant passages from your indexed knowledge sources within milliseconds.

Gemini 3.1 Flash Synthesis

Generates interactive self-grading quizzes, tactile 3D flip flashcards, and teacher-like explanations strictly anchored to your uploaded syllabus.

Native Export Engines

Automated generation of publication-ready PowerPoint (.pptx) presentations and vectorized ReportLab (.pdf) study guides.

Hi! I'm Abhinav M (Abhinav Muralidhar), a full-stack and AI developer passionate about engineering software that reduces cognitive fatigue and makes learning rewarding.

I built EduWise to tackle a personal pain point experienced during intense exam cycles: spending hours creating study questions, summarizing textbook chapters, and preparing presentation slides instead of actually engaging with the concepts.

Rather than treating AI as a generic novelty chat box, I wanted to build a cohesive, tactile learning environment that treats user documents as the authoritative ground truth. Every design decision—from the tactile card borders and warm paper aesthetic to the custom pgvector dual-dialect storage—was chosen to make serious learning focused, reliable, and distraction-free.
