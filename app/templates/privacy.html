Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Effective for all EduWise users

[01] Overview & Philosophy

EduWise is designed with privacy and student trust as foundational principles. We do not sell your personal data, monetize your study habits, or share your documents with data brokers. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is processed, and the measures we take to protect it.

[02] Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your chosen username and email address. Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and unique per-user salts.
  • Uploaded Study Documents: Files you upload (PDF, DOCX, TXT) to the Knowledge Base are temporarily parsed into text chunks and converted into vector embeddings.
  • Generated Resources: Quizzes, flashcards, presentation files, and summaries you create are saved to your private dashboard library for ongoing review.

[03] How Your Study Materials Are Used

Your study documents are used solely to generate your requested learning materials via Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):

Private to your account: Chunks and vector embeddings are strictly partitioned by your user_id. No other user can search or retrieve your uploaded documents.

Not used for AI training: Text sent to Google Gemini APIs is processed solely for content generation and is not used to train public foundation models.

[04] Cookies & Local Storage

EduWise uses secure HTTP session cookies strictly for session authentication and CSRF protection. We also store non-sensitive aesthetic preferences (such as your chosen Light/Dark theme) in your browser's localStorage. We utilize privacy-conscious Google Analytics (gtag.js) to monitor site uptime and high-level feature engagement.

[05] Your Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain complete ownership of your data at all times:

  • You can delete individual Knowledge Sources or generated resources directly from your workspace dashboard at any time.
  • You can permanently delete your entire account from your Profile settings. Doing so triggers a complete cascade deletion that immediately erases your account, credentials, uploaded sources, chunks, and generated files from the database.

[06] Contact & Questions

If you have questions about this privacy notice or data handling within EduWise, feel free to reach out via GitHub at github.com/Abhinav-Muralidhar.
